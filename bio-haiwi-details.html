Bio-Haiwi App

A comprehensive biology terminology application in both Arabic and English with advanced search features, favorites, and data synchronization

App Design

About the Project

Bio-Haiwi is a specialized application for biology terminology in both Arabic and English. The app provides a comprehensive educational experience with fast search capabilities, saving favorite terms, and data synchronization between devices. It also supports offline mode to ensure access to information at any time.

Arabic and English Language Support

The app fully supports both Arabic and English languages, making it suitable for all users whether they speak Arabic, English, or both.

Advanced Search

A fast and effective search feature that allows users to easily find any term whether searching in Arabic or English.

Term Management (Admin Only)

An integrated management system that allows only admins to add and modify terms, ensuring content accuracy and quality. An easy and comfortable interface for content management.

Offline Mode

The app works fully in offline mode, meaning users can access all terms even without an internet connection. Data is stored locally on the device to ensure continuity of use.

App Logo

A professional logo that reflects the app's educational identity and focus on biology terminology.
